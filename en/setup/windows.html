Install Hossam TV on Windows PC & laptop

Free SFVIP Player. About 5 minutes, any plan.

Golden rule: one screen at a time. Install on as many devices as you like, but only one can play at once — watching on two at the same time can lock the account.

Steps

  1. 1
    Download SFVIP Player (ZIP file) from MediaFire.Click the big blue Download button — not "Download faster".
  2. 2
    Right-click the ZIP file, choose Extract All, then open the app inside.If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  3. 3
    Sign in with the username and password we sent you on WhatsApp, and the server URL for your plan:
